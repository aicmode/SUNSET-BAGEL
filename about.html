Our story

Hand rolled by the coast.

Santa Monica state of mind

Born from beach mornings, corner cafes, and the easy rhythm of Los Angeles.

SUNSET BAGEL is a California take on the neighborhood bagel shop. We borrow the care and chew of a classic bake, then soften the mood with bright produce, clean design, fresh coffee, and a little surf culture.

Everything begins before sunrise: dough mixed in small batches, bagels shaped by hand, toppings prepped with market vegetables, and espresso dialed in while the street is still quiet.

Warm minimal

Simple food, generous texture, and a room that feels like morning.

We keep the palette calm and the menu focused: chewy bagels, seasonal sandwiches, crisp salads, citrus, herbs, and coffee that fits the pace of a westside day.
